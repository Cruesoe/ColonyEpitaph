# Colony Epitaph

RimWorld 1.6 mod.

When your colony is wiped out, the game fades to a full-screen epitaph instead of a letter.

It remembers the colony's name, how long it lasted, the date it fell, and the colonists who lived and died there. Click any of them to read their story.

## What you see

When everyone is dead, kidnapped, or gone, the map dims and the game pauses:

- The colony name, and how the story ended
- How long the run lasted, and the date the colony fell
- Wealth, peak population, and raids survived
- Portraits of the colony: role and title holders on the top row, then everyone else by time served, living and dead together
- The deceased in ashen tones so they read apart from the living at a glance
- **+N more** for anyone who does not fit on screen
- The same choices vanilla already offers: keep watching, create wanderers, load a save, main menu

Victory credits, the planetkiller fade, and Man in Black are left alone.

## Colonist details

Click any portrait, living or dead, for a panel covering:

- Name, age, role or royal title, and time spent in the colony
- The date and cause of death, for those who did not make it
- Backstory and traits
- All twelve skills in the usual order, with passions
- Family, including children and siblings; click a name to jump to them
- Notable deeds drawn from their records

## Options

**Options → Mod options → Colony Epitaph** restores the original Game Over letter.

## Previewing it

To see the screen without wiping a colony, enable development mode and use **Debug actions → Colony Epitaph → Open epitaph screen**.
