[b]When your colony is wiped out, the game fades to a full-screen epitaph instead of a letter.[/b]

I love feedback, so if there are stats you'd like to see on your colony's final page, let me know in the comments below!

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]The epitaph remembers the colony's name, how long it lasted, the date it fell, and everyone who lived and died there.
[*]Clicking any portrait opens that colonist's story: backstory, traits, skills, family, and the deeds that defined them.
[*]The usual choices remain: keep watching, create wanderers, or return to the menu.
[*]A debug action, Colony Epitaph > Open epitaph screen, previews the epitaph without waiting for your colony to die.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Use vanilla game over letter[/b]: shows the original Game Over letter instead of the epitaph. Off by default.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save.
